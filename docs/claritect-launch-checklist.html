Product — Demo must be flawless Priority 1

  • Demo database with sample data — pre-loaded Postgres with realistic sales, finance, pipeline, support tables. 5-6 tables minimum.
  • One-click demo flow — prospect clicks "Try a live demo" → sees a real question answered against sample data → report in under 60 seconds.
  • Connection onboarding wizard — paste connection string → discover tables → confirm scope → done. Must feel safe and fast.
  • Read-only enforcement visible in UI — show the SQL that ran. Show it's SELECT only. This is the #1 objection. Address it before they ask.
  • Report quality at consultant level — professional formatting, charts, findings, and actionable recommendations. This is what they pay for.
  • PDF export working — every report downloadable as a polished PDF they can forward to their board.
  • Email delivery — reports delivered to inbox, not just viewable in the app.
  • Scheduled reports end-to-end — set a weekly schedule, receive report on time. This is the sticky feature that prevents churn.
  • Follow-up / clarification flow — after a report, user asks "Why did churn spike in March?" and gets a grounded follow-up.
  • Error handling — LLM failure, DB timeout, or empty results show a professional message, never a stack trace.
  • Progress indicators — for a 20-30s pipeline, show: "Scoping your question..." → "Analyzing data..." → "Building report..." Not a spinner.
  • Mobile-responsive reports — executives read reports on their phone at 11pm.

Security & trust Priority 1

  • SSL/TLS everywhere — HTTPS on all endpoints. Encrypted database connections.
  • Read-only SQL guarantee documented — written policy and technical enforcement. Visible in the product.
  • Data handling policy — what do you store? Query results? For how long? Write it down, publish it.
  • Query audit log — every SQL query run, when, by whom, visible to the customer. Your governance differentiator.
  • Terms of Service — reviewed by a lawyer. Covers data handling, liability, SLA commitments.
  • Privacy Policy — GDPR-aware even outside EU. Your customers' customers may be in the EU.
  • Security one-pager PDF — 1-page answering: "How does Claritect handle our data?" Pre-empts the security review.
  • SOC 2 readiness — not SOC 2 itself yet. Access logging, encryption at rest, audit trail. Document your practices.

Infrastructure — Production-ready Priority 1

  • Production deployment — proper VPS or cloud instance with a deploy pipeline. Not running on your laptop.
  • Custom domain — app.claritect.ai or similar. Not a platform subdomain.
  • Automated daily backups — DB snapshots. Test a restore at least once before launch.
  • Uptime monitoring — Uptime Robot (free) or Better Uptime. Know before your customer does.
  • Error alerting — Sentry or similar. Get notified when the API, LLM, or worker breaks.
  • Usage metering per tenant — track queries per tenant per month. Required for caps and overage billing.
  • Rate limiting per tenant — prevent one user from burning your entire OpenRouter quota.
  • Environment separation — production + staging at minimum. Never test on the live system.

Website & marketing Priority 2

  • Marketing homepage — in progress
  • Pricing page — in progress
  • One-pager pitch PDF — done
  • 3 example reports on website — a finance report, an ops report, and a business case. Show what Claritect delivers. These sell harder than copy.
  • 30-second product video — screen recording: question typed → report appears. Embed on homepage hero.
  • 2-3 minute demo video — full walkthrough for people who want depth. YouTube + embed on site.
  • Blog with 2-3 SEO articles — "How to automate executive reporting", "AI vs hiring a data analyst", etc.
  • Favicon, Open Graph tags, meta descriptions — looks professional when shared on Slack, Twitter, email previews.

Sales process Priority 1

  • Pilot booking flow — "Book a Live Pilot" → Calendly or Cal.com → 30-min slot. Zero friction.
  • Pilot demo script — what you walk through in 30 minutes. Rehearsed 5+ times. Demo with sample data first, then offer to connect their data live.
  • 5 killer demo questions prepared — Finance: margin shift. Ops: supplier underperformance. Product: release impact. Strategy: APAC business case. Scheduled: weekly pipeline summary.
  • Backup demo recording — if live demo fails (LLM timeout), switch to a pre-recorded walkthrough seamlessly.
  • Follow-up email template — sent within 1 hour of pilot. Summary of what you showed, link to their report, pricing, next steps.
  • Proposal template — 1-page PDF: what they get, price, timeline, terms. Not a 20-page SOW.
  • ROI calculation ready — "You spend X on consulting today. Claritect gives you the same for $499/month." Have the math on screen.
  • CRM — even a spreadsheet. Track: who, what stage, follow-up date. Graduate to HubSpot free at 20+ leads.

Legal & billing Priority 2

  • Business entity registered — you're charging $500+/month. You need a proper company (LLC / Ltd).
  • Business bank account — separate from personal. Required for Stripe payouts.
  • Stripe account verified — test a real payment end-to-end. Set up the 3 subscription plans.
  • Professional invoices — Stripe generates these, but verify they have your logo and look right.
  • Cancellation flow — how does a customer cancel? Make it easy. Don't be the product that's hard to leave.
  • Refund policy — "Not happy in the first 30 days? Full refund." Reduces purchase risk significantly.
  • Contract template — simple SaaS agreement. Month-to-month to reduce friction. Annual discount (2 months free) available.

Post-launch — first 30 days Priority 3

  • Customer onboarding email sequence — after sign-up: connect DB → first report → set up schedule → add team members. Automated 4-email drip.
  • Weekly check-in with first 5 customers — 15-min call. "What's working? What's not?" This feedback is gold.
  • Usage dashboard for yourself — see per-tenant: queries this month, LLM cost, reports generated. Spot problems before customers complain.
  • Collect testimonials — ask happy customers for a 2-sentence quote. Use on website and PH listing.

Execution order

Don't try to do everything at once. Ship in this order:

Week 1-2: Foundation
  • Demo database + demo flow
  • Production deployment + domain
  • Stripe billing
  • Pilot booking (Calendly)
  • Pilot script rehearsed
Week 3-4: Trust & credibility
  • Security one-pager
  • Terms of Service + Privacy Policy
  • 3 example reports on website
  • Product video (30s + 3min)
  • Usage metering + rate limiting
Week 5-6: Go to market
  • Product Hunt launch
  • Show HN post
  • Cold email batch 1
  • Community posting begins
  • Google Ads live
Week 7-8: Iterate
  • First customer onboarded
  • Weekly check-ins running
  • Blog posts + SEO
  • Newsletter sponsorship
  • Collect first testimonials
